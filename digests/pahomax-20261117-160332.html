How this report is made
  • Stockfish evaluates every position. Evaluations are always from your side: positive is good for you.
  • A move is a mistake when your winning chances drop by 20 points or more, and a blunder at 30.
  • "Recurring" means the same kind of mistake in at least 3 different games. Anything less is marked "Notable".
  • "Under 10% clock" means at most a tenth of the starting time was left. It is context, not a verdict.
  • "The game went" is what was actually played. Engine lines are what Stockfish would play.
  • *Accuracy uses the Lichess win-chance formula, so it differs from chess.com's CAPS score.
  • Red arrow: your move. Green arrow: the engine's move. Click a move in any line to see that position (← → step through it); click your move to go back.
